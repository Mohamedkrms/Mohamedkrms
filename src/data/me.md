I maintain a personal homelab and edge infrastructure to experiment with Linux administration, DNS, private networking, and automated AI workloads.

My setup includes a dedicated Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W alongside additional homelab compute nodes used for hands-on engineering experiments.

- **Raspberry Pi AI Automation Node:** Runs scheduled AI blog generation pipelines, task runners, and automated data scripts operating 24/7 on low-power ARM architecture.
- **Pi-hole:** Network-wide DNS filtering, custom internal DNS routing, and ad blocking.
- **DNS resolution:** Configuring and troubleshooting upstream DNS services and encrypted DNS (DoH/DoT).
- **Tailscale:** Encrypted zero-trust private remote access connecting my development machines, homelab nodes, and cloud servers.
- **Linux Administration:** System configuration, command-line administration, automated systemd services, and troubleshooting on Arch Linux and Ubuntu/Debian Server.
- **Self-hosting & Automation:** Exploring how modern services are orchestrated, monitored, and maintained in production-like environments.

The homelab provides a practical proving ground for understanding networking protocols, infrastructure resilience, and self-hosted AI automation under real-world constraints.
